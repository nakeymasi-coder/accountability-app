Install on your phone

Add Accountability to your home screen. Your account and saved commitments stay on the server when you switch devices. An internet connection is needed to load updates and save changes.

Android · Chrome

  1. Open this app in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.
  4. Tap the Accountability icon on your home screen to open it.

iPhone · Safari

  1. Open this app in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Notifications and reminders

Open the app and tap Notifications for unread requests, supporters, and commitment reminders. Email reminders arrive even while this app is closed if you turn them on in Account. Phone push notifications are not included.

My private Google Calendar

      
Connect your own Google account to automatically sync requests you post and commitments you join into a separate Glam Accountability calendar. Updates sync about every five minutes. The app does not read your other calendars or add other members as guests.

      

      

      
Only you can connect or disconnect your Google account. Disconnect stops future syncing; events already in Google stay there. Keep the calendar private in Google Calendar’s sharing settings. Community requests remain visible to signed-in members inside this app.
